AI-assisted resume screening for HR teams

Hireflow is a web application that helps HR and recruiting teams collect candidate documents, screen resumes against a job description, and get answers from their own hiring documents in plain language.

What Hireflow does

  • Document library Upload resumes, job descriptions, and policy documents. Hireflow extracts the text so every file becomes searchable.
  • Search and Q&A Ask questions across the whole library and get an answer with citations back to the source document.
  • Resume screening Score and rank candidates against the requirements of an open role, with an explanation of why each candidate matched.
  • Gmail integration Optionally connect one or more Gmail mailboxes so resume attachments emailed to your recruiting inbox are pulled in automatically. Read-only — Hireflow can never send, modify, or delete anything in your mail.

The Gmail connection

Connecting Gmail is optional and is never required to use Hireflow. When you do connect a mailbox, Hireflow asks Google for a single permission — read-only access — so it can find resume attachments. It requests no permission to send, modify, or delete mail, and holds none. Hireflow downloads only PDF and Word attachments, and you can disconnect a mailbox at any time from the app's settings page, which revokes Hireflow's access with Google immediately.

Our Privacy Policy explains exactly what Google user data Hireflow accesses, what it stores, and how to have it deleted.
