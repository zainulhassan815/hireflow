Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Hireflow ("Hireflow", "we", "us") is a resume screening and document search application operated by DreamersLab. This policy explains what data the application collects, how it is used, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted. It applies to the Hireflow web application at hireflow.dreamerslab.dev.

Data we collect

CategoryWhat it isWhy we need it
Account data Your name, email address, and a hashed password. To create your account, sign you in, and attribute actions in the audit log.
Documents you provide Resumes, job descriptions, and other files you upload or that Hireflow ingests from a connected mailbox, plus the text extracted from them. To make documents searchable, to screen candidates against a role, and to answer your questions with citations.
Google account data See the Google user data section below. Only if you choose to connect a Gmail mailbox.
Activity logs Records of actions taken in the app — uploads, searches, syncs, connections and disconnections — with a timestamp and IP address. Security, troubleshooting, and giving your organisation an audit trail.

Google user data

Connecting a Gmail mailbox is entirely optional. Hireflow works without it. If you choose to connect one, you are shown a Google consent screen listing the permissions below, and you grant them yourself.

Permissions we request and why

ScopeWhat it lets Hireflow doWhy we request it
openid, email Read the email address of the mailbox you are connecting. So the app can show you which mailbox is connected and let you connect more than one.
gmail.readonly Read your messages and download their attachments. This is the only Gmail permission Hireflow requests. To find messages that carry a resume and pull the attachment into your Hireflow document library. Hireflow cannot send, modify, or delete anything in your mailbox.

What Hireflow actually reads and stores

Hireflow queries your mailbox on a schedule for recent messages that have attachments. For each such message it downloads only attachments that are PDF or Microsoft Word files, because those are the formats resumes arrive in. From that process it stores:

  • the attachment files themselves, and the text extracted from them, in your Hireflow document library;
  • the Gmail message identifier of each message it has already processed, so the same resume is not imported twice;
  • the connected mailbox's email address, and the timestamp of the last sync.

Hireflow does not store the body text of your emails, your subject lines, your contact list, attachments of any other file type, or messages that carry no attachment.

Storage of Google credentials

Hireflow stores the OAuth refresh token Google issues for the connected mailbox, encrypted at rest, so that background syncing can continue without asking you to sign in repeatedly. Access tokens are held only in memory for the duration of a single sync and are never written to disk.

Limited Use disclosure

Hireflow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Concretely: we do not sell Google user data, we do not use it for advertising, we do not transfer it to third parties except the service providers listed below that are necessary to operate the app, and we do not allow humans to read it except with your explicit permission, where required for security or to comply with the law, or on data that has been aggregated and anonymised.

Google user data is not used to train any generative AI or machine learning model, by us or by our service providers.

How we use the data

  • To operate the features you asked for: search, question answering, candidate screening, and mailbox sync.
  • To secure the service, investigate abuse, and diagnose faults.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Service providers

Hireflow uses a small number of third parties to function. They process data only on our instructions.

  • Anthropic — text from your documents, and your questions, are sent to the Claude API to generate answers, summaries, and screening explanations. Anthropic does not train its models on data submitted through its API.
  • Google — the Gmail API, only for a mailbox you have connected.
  • Our hosting and infrastructure providers — for the servers, database, and file storage that run the application.

Document embeddings and search indexing are computed on our own infrastructure and are not sent to a third party.

Retention and deletion

  • Disconnecting a mailbox. Disconnecting a Gmail account in Hireflow's settings revokes our refresh token with Google and deletes the stored connection. Hireflow immediately loses all access to that mailbox. Documents already imported remain in your library until you delete them.
  • Revoking from Google. You can also revoke Hireflow's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Hireflow detects the revocation on its next sync and removes the connection on its own.
  • Deleting documents. Deleting a document removes the file, its extracted text, and its search index entries.
  • Deleting your account. Email dreamerslabdev@gmail.com and we will delete your account and all associated documents and connections within 30 days.
  • Activity logs are retained for up to 12 months for security and audit purposes.

Security

Traffic to Hireflow is encrypted in transit with TLS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted at rest. Access to production systems is restricted to the people who operate the service. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these, email dreamerslabdev@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Where Hireflow is used by an employer to screen candidates, that employer is the controller of the candidate data they upload, and candidate requests should be directed to them in the first instance.

Children

Hireflow is a workplace tool and is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change materially affects how we handle Google user data, we will notify account holders by email before it takes effect.
